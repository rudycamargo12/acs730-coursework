Conclusion:
Both methods successfully deployed an Apache web server.
The Golden AMI was ready in 21 seconds because Apache was
preinstalled and enabled during image creation.

The Ansible method required configuration after instance launch.
Its 178-second measurement includes manual pauses, SSM readiness,
playbook execution, and HTTP verification.

These results demonstrate the operational difference between
baking configuration into an AMI and configuring after launch,
but they are not a controlled performance benchmark.
